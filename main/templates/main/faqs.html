FAQs

Who can attend?

The conference is open to young leaders, policymakers, civil society, development partners and the private sector. Everyone is welcome to participate and contribute.

How do I register for the conference?

You can register by clicking the “Register Now” button on the homepage. Fill out the registration form with your details and submit. You’ll receive a confirmation email once your registration is complete.

Is there a fee?

Registration fee depends on your registration package. However, participants are required to register ahead of the event to secure a spot and receive access to conference sessions and materials.

How can I become a speaker or panelist?

If you're interested in speaking or presenting, kindly reach out via our Contact Us page or email us at conference@wearepeople.org Speaker applications may be open for a limited time.

Can I volunteer for the conference?

Yes! Volunteers are always welcome. Please fill out the Volunteer Sign-Up Form on the registration page or contact us directly for more information.

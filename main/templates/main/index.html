ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

We Are People Conference is a national youth-focused event hosted by YouthRISE Nigeria, celebrating the role of young Nigerians in nation-building while tackling the barriers that limit their full participation. The conference convenes young leaders, policymakers, civil society, development partners, and the private sector to highlight youth excellence, promote unity, and drive inclusive national development. The We Are People Conference is more than just a conference, it's a movement, a platform to uplift and empower every young Nigerians, embracing diversity, resilience, and collaboration to unlock the full potential of Nigerians youth.

Uniting Voices, Igniting Change

A Platform where young leaders, policymakers, and changemakers gathers to shape Nigeria’s future.

The We Are People Conference is more than a gathering — it’s a movement of vibrant youth voices, dynamic conversations, and powerful collaborations. From thought-provoking keynotes to hands-on skill-building sessions, the conference creates space for innovation, unity, and action. With a diverse mix of perspectives across governance, entrepreneurship, digital transformation, and social development, participants will leave equipped, inspired, and connected to drive real impact in their communities and beyond.


Celebrating the Power of Youth in Nation-Building

Uniting young Nigerians with leaders and innovators to inspire action, spark collaboration, and shape a stronger future

The We Are People Conference is a vibrant platform that recognizes the creativity, resilience, and leadership of young Nigerians. It goes beyond dialogue, creating opportunities for collaboration, mentorship, and innovation that break down barriers to youth participation. By bringing together diverse stakeholders — from government and civil society to private sector leaders and young changemakers — the conference fosters unity and drives collective solutions that will shape a more inclusive and prosperous Nigeria.

ABOUT THE CONFERENCE

We Are People Conference is a national youth-focused event hosted by YouthRISE Nigeria, celebrating the role of young Nigerians in nation-building while tackling the barriers that limit their full participation. The conference convenes young leaders, policymakers, civil society, development partners, and the private sector to highlight youth excellence, promote unity, and drive inclusive national development. The We Are People Conference is more than just a conference, it's a movement, a platform to uplift and empower every young Nigerians, embracing diversity, resilience, and collaboration to unlock the full potential of Nigerians youth.

CONFERENCE STRUCTURE
Keynote Address:
Distinguished speakers with expertise in youth engagement, entrepreneurship and leadership will deliver an address to set the thematic tone for the conference. The keynote speaker will provide insights into the role of young people in nation-building, leadership ethics, and the responsibilities of active citizenship.
Plenary Sessions:
Involving expert-led discussions on critical issues affecting Nigerian youths, such as political participation, digital transformation, education reform, and socio-economic development. Breakout & Skill Building Sessions: Thematic deep dives into specific areas of entrepreneurship, drug use, human rights, youth development and empowerment, education, and governance. Here, participants will engage leading experts in meaningful discussions, share insights, and proffer solutions to identified challenges. Networking Sessions: Dedicated sessions to foster collaborations and mentorship opportunities between young changemakers, mentors, and key stakeholders. Youth-Led Exhibitions: Dedicated sessions to foster collaborations and mentorship opportunities between young changemakers, mentors, and key stakeholders.

What you’ll discover at the conference


How young Nigerians play a pivotal role in shaping the future of the nation, and understand the ethics and responsibilities of active citizenship.

Strategies for impactful leadership, entrepreneurial growth, and how to harness innovation for personal and national development.



Sessions covering critical issues such as political participation, education reform, digital transformation, and socio-economic development.

Youth-led exhibitions that showcase creativity, resilience, and innovative solutions from young Nigerians across diverse fields.
